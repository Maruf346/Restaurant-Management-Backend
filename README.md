# Restaurant Management Backend

A robust backend system designed for restaurant operations, built to support day-to-day management across multiple locations and business workflows.

This project is intended to power a modern restaurant platform that can manage ordering, inventory, sales tracking, menu operations, reporting, and notifications in a centralized way.

It is designed to support both web-based administration and mobile-friendly usage, helping restaurant teams streamline operations while maintaining visibility into stock, revenue, performance, and operational alerts.

The system focuses on practical business needs such as:

- managing restaurant locations and branch-specific data
- tracking ingredients and stock levels
- organizing menu items and recipe details
- monitoring daily sales and item performance
- generating operational insights and analytics
- sending alerts for important business events
- supporting integrations with POS systems and related services

The goal is to provide a reliable foundation for a restaurant management platform that can scale as operations grow and support real-time visibility across teams.
